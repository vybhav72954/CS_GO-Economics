PHASE 3 FINAL SUMMARY
======================================================================

RESEARCH QUESTION:
  Is momentum in CS:GO a psychological phenomenon or an economic artifact?

DATA:
  Stockholm Major 2021, 1587 rounds from 65 matches

BASELINE FINDING:
  Winning previous round multiplies current round win odds by 3.25x
  This appears to be strong "momentum" or "hot hand" effect

DECOMPOSITION RESULTS:

  Panel A (Categorical Controls - Regime Dummies):
    Baseline:              coef = 1.1777, OR = 3.25x, p < 0.001 ***
    + Regime:              coef = 0.0760, OR = 1.08x, p = 0.592
    + Regime + Maps:       coef = 0.0481, OR = 1.05x, p = 0.736
    + Regime + Maps + Ph:  coef = 0.0611, OR = 1.06x, p = 0.673

  Panel B (Continuous Controls - Equipment $):
    Baseline:              coef = 1.1777, OR = 3.25x, p < 0.001 ***
    + Equipment:           coef = -0.2497, OR = 0.78x, p = 0.090
    + Equipment + Maps:    coef = -0.2771, OR = 0.76x, p = 0.062
    + Equip + Maps + Ph:   coef = -0.2882, OR = 0.75x, p = 0.053

ROBUSTNESS:
  Both teams full buy (n=1000): momentum coef = 0.1508, p = 0.245
  Gun rounds only (n=1300): after equipment control, coef = -0.1802

CONCLUSION:
  The apparent momentum effect (OR = 3.25x) is fully explained by economics.
  
  - With categorical controls (regime dummies): coefficient drops to ~0, NOT significant
  - With continuous controls (equipment $): coefficient becomes slightly NEGATIVE
  - When both teams have full equipment: momentum is NOT significant (p = 0.245)
  
  Momentum in CS:GO is an ECONOMIC ILLUSION created by the loss bonus system,
  not a psychological phenomenon. What looks like "hot hand" is simply winners
  having more money to buy better equipment.
